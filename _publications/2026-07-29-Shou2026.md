This work formalizes an engineer-like workflow for PID tuning in chemical processes: observe closed-loop responses, diagnose control deficiencies, adjust gains, and validate the result. Hosted language models use response features, tuning preferences, and IMC-based demonstrations to recommend and iteratively correct PID gains. For local deployment, Qwen3-0.6B is adapted through supervised fine-tuning with simulation-verified IMC targets and physics-informed group relative policy optimization with stability and performance rewards. Experiments on first- and second-order time-delay processes assess tuning success and first-attempt reliability.
