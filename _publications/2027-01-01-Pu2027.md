This work introduces an orthogonal collocation physics-informed neural network (OCPINN) for simulating nonlinear and multiscale chemical kinetics. The network predicts time-varying coefficients of a Lagrange interpolation basis constructed at Gauss-Legendre nodes, providing a structured solution representation while retaining automatic differentiation. Across a nonlinear consecutive reaction, the stiff Robertson problem, and an industrial methanol-to-propylene fixed-bed reactor, OCPINN achieved the lowest mean state-scaled global root mean square error among the PINN methods compared in the paper.
